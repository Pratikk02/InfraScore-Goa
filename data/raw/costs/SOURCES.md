# Facility cost sources (Rs crore)

`facility_costs.csv` lists, for each facility type, the capital cost and its source, and the annual operating cost and its basis.
Health operating costs come from Prinja et al. (2016), PLoS ONE 11(8): e0160986 (2012-13 prices), uprated by the All-India CPI
(Combined, base 2012 = 100; January 2025 = 193.5, MoSPI). The fire-station operating cost is the DFES revenue budget for 2025-26
(Rs 91.87 crore, Goa Budget Vol. III, Demand No. 26) divided by 17 stations. Operating costs marked "assumption" have no published
Goa figure and are varied in the Monte Carlo (log-normal, sigma = 0.4) together with every other cost.
